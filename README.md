# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

---

## Introduction

*Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban* is the third film in the Harry Potter series. It follows Harry's third year at Hogwarts, when a dangerous escaped prisoner is rumored to be hunting him. The film is darker and more mysterious than its two predecessors and deals with themes of fear, loyalty, and the truth behind old accusations. It was directed by Alfonso Cuarón and based on the novel by J.K. Rowling.

- **Genre:** Fantasy / Adventure
- **Release year:** 2004

---

## Synopsis

Harry's summer with the Dursleys ends badly when he loses his temper and runs away, only to learn that **Sirius Black**, a supposed follower of Voldemort, has escaped from the wizard prison Azkaban. Dementors are posted around Hogwarts to catch him, but their presence affects Harry more than anyone else. A new teacher, Professor Lupin, helps Harry learn the Patronus Charm to defend himself against them. Harry later discovers that Sirius is his godfather and that he was blamed for betraying Harry's parents. In the Shrieking Shack, the truth comes out: the real traitor is Peter Pettigrew, who has been hiding as Ron's pet rat, Scabbers. With Hermione's Time-Turner, Harry and Hermione go back in time to save the hippogriff Buckbeak and help Sirius escape.

### Story Highlights

1. Harry learns of Sirius Black's escape
2. The Dementors arrive at Hogwarts
3. Lupin teaches Harry the Patronus Charm
4. The truth is revealed in the Shrieking Shack
5. Harry and Hermione use the Time-Turner
